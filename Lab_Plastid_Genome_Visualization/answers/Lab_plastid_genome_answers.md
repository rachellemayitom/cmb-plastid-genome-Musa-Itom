# Lab Plastid Genome Visualization — Answers

## 1. Scientific name and plastid genome accession number

The scientific name of my chosen plant is *Musa itinerans*, and its plastid genome accession number is **NC_035723.1**.

## 2. Total length of the plastid genome

The total length of the plastid genome is **168,985 bp**.

## 3. LSC, SSC, IRa, and IRb regions

Yes. The **LSC** region is located at the upper portion of the map, while the **SSC** region is at the lower portion. **IRa** is located at the lower-right side, and **IRb** is located at the lower-left side. The two IR regions separate the LSC and SSC regions.

## 4. Three genes located in the LSC region

Three examples of genes located in the LSC region are ***rbcL, psbA, and atpA***.

## 5. Gene located in the SSC region

One example of a gene located in the SSC region is ***ndhF***.

## 6. Gene located within an inverted repeat region

One example is ***rpl2***. Yes, it occurs more than once because the two inverted repeat regions contain duplicated copies of genes.

## 7. Photosynthesis-related gene

The ***rbcL*** gene is a photosynthesis-related gene that encodes the large subunit of Rubisco. Rubisco is involved in **carbon fixation during photosynthesis**.

## 8. Direction of gene arrows and transcription

The direction of the arrows indicates the **direction in which the genes are transcribed**. Genes pointing in opposite directions are transcribed from opposite DNA strands.

## 9. GC content

The GC content does **not appear completely uniform** across the plastid genome. The graph shows fluctuations in GC content at different positions, indicating that some regions have relatively higher or lower GC content than others.

## 10. Usefulness of a graphical plastid genome map

A graphical plastid genome map makes it easier to **visualize the genome organization, locations of genes, LSC, SSC, and IR regions, gene orientation, and GC content**. It allows structural features and gene distribution to be understood more quickly than from a raw DNA sequence or a long list of annotations.
