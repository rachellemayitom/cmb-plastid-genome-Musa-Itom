## 3. Plastid Genome Summary

The chloroplast genome of *Musa itinerans* is 168,985 bp long with an overall GC content of 36.82%. It has a typical quadripartite structure consisting of a Large Single-Copy (LSC) region, a Small Single-Copy (SSC) region, and two Inverted Repeat (IR) regions.

- **LSC:** 87,828 bp
- **SSC:** 11,329 bp
- **IR:** 34,914 bp each
- **Overall GC content:** 36.82%

The genome follows the typical arrangement:

**LSC – IR – SSC – IR**

## 4. Genome Retrieval and Galaxy Analysis

The complete chloroplast genome of *Musa itinerans* was downloaded from NCBI in FASTA and GenBank formats. The FASTA file was then uploaded to the Galaxy platform for sequence statistics.

The Galaxy history used for the analysis was named:

**Plastid_Musa_Itom**

The FASTA Statistics tool was used to determine the sequence length and GC content.

The Galaxy analysis produced the following results:

- **Number of sequences:** 1
- **Total sequence length:** 168,985 bp
- **GC content:** 36.82%

Screenshots of the Galaxy history, uploaded sequence, and sequence-statistics results are included in the `figures/` folder.

## 5. Gene Content and Important Observations

The current NCBI annotation contains 133 gene features, including 88 protein-coding sequences, 37 tRNA genes, and 8 rRNA genes. No pseudogenes are annotated in the current record.

The chloroplast genome contains genes involved in photosynthesis, transcription, translation, and electron transport. Examples include *psaA*, *psbA*, *atpA*, *petA*, *rbcL*, *rpoB*, *rps12*, and *ndhA*.

Several genes contain introns, including *rps16*, *atpF*, *rpoC1*, *ycf3*, *clpP*, *petB*, *petD*, *rpl2*, *ndhB*, and *ndhA*.

A notable feature of the genome is the presence of duplicated genes within the inverted-repeat regions.
